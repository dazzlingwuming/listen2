## Acceptance and constraints

- Cached analysis is refreshed at safe playback boundaries, with source/hash/version identity preserved and no async stale response affecting a different song.
- Recoverable analysis failures can retry within a bounded policy; permanent unsupported/resource failures stay explicit.
- Silence does not trigger runaway gain; unknown material has bounded adaptation; known analysis supplies stable initial gain. Explicit ramp/reset behavior covers toggle, seek, pause and track changes.
- Output limiting and final metering happen at an agreed point after FX, with no double processing, duplicate audible paths or silence regression. Preserve user volume/mute and MV audio synchronization.
- FX toggles preserve tone changes and remove gratuitous fixed loudness loss; quantitative original-versus-FX measurements must support the claim.
- Independent standard reference or published reference fixtures validate the meter; do not label a compressor/soft clipper as a true-peak limiter without measurement evidence.
- Isolated synthetic/offline verification does not imply real-user song listening or all-platform support. Report remaining limits honestly.
